Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between Calm Mode and Game Mode?

Tap the toggle at the top of the screen. Calm Mode is a relaxing, endless experience with no lives or pressure. Game Mode adds combos, special bubbles, and an escalating challenge as you climb.

Is my data collected?

No. Bubble Burst Therapy does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything happens right on your device.

The haptics or sound aren't working — what should I do?

Make sure your device isn't in silent mode and that system haptics are enabled in Settings. If your device is in Low Power Mode, haptics may be reduced. If the issue continues, reach out via the button above.

Which languages are supported?

Bubble Burst Therapy is available in English, Spanish, and French, and automatically matches your device's language.

Privacy

Bubble Burst Therapy collects no personal data whatsoever. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads. Your gameplay stays entirely on your device.
